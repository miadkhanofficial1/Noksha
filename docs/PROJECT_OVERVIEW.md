# Project Overview — Noksha (নকশা)

**Noksha (নকশা)** is a modern, full-stack, AI-inspired Digital Graphics Template Marketplace designed to empower Bangladeshi and international UI/UX designers, vector artists, and graphic content creators. Built with **Laravel 12** and **Bootstrap 5**, Noksha offers an end-to-end digital ecosystem featuring asset publishing, multi-role security, identity verification (KYC), design contests, real-time notifications, local keyword AI auto-tagging, and an executive administration console.

---

## 1. Problem Statement
Digital content creators in Bangladesh and emerging regional markets face significant hurdles when distributing and monetizing their digital graphics assets:
- **Lack of Local Marketplace Infrastructure:** Creators rely on heavy international platforms with high payout thresholds and complex verification.
- **Inadequate Search & Discovery:** Manual tagging leads to poor discoverability for niche localized design templates (e.g., Bengali typography, regional festival vectors).
- **Security & Quality Risks:** Unverified uploads often expose buyers to low-quality or stolen assets.
- **Limited Community Engagement:** Lack of incentive structures for creative design challenges and creator leaderboards.

---

## 2. The Noksha Solution
Noksha solves these challenges by providing:
- **Localized Digital Asset Marketplace:** Instant publishing for PSD templates, Figma UI kits, SVG vectors, and 3D assets.
- **AI-Inspired Local Keyword Auto-Tagging:** Automatically extracts and indexes relevant design keywords (`#ui`, `#fintech`, `#mobile`, `#dashboard`, `#figma`) without external paid API dependencies.
- **Pro Creator KYC Verification:** Multi-step identity verification (Government ID, Selfie, Video) ensuring high-quality, verified authors.
- **Interactive Design Contests Arena:** Sponsored design challenges with cash prize pools, participant submission galleries, and Top 10 Creator Leaderboards.
- **Real-Time Notification Center:** In-app alert system notifying users of sales, approvals, winner announcements, and order status updates.
- **Executive Administration Console:** Executive dashboard with Chart.js analytics, metric counters, user status toggles (Suspend/Activate), and system-wide broadcast alerts.

---

## 3. Key Objectives
1. **High Performance & Scalability:** Deliver sub-second page loads using Laravel 12 Eloquent optimization, eager loading, and Vite production bundling.
2. **Robust Multi-Role Security:** Strict role-based authorization for Buyers, Sellers, and Super Admins.
3. **Seamless Digital Delivery:** Secure, direct file streaming for purchased digital resources.
4. **Responsive Purple Glassmorphism Aesthetics:** Modern design system built exclusively with Bootstrap 5 and custom CSS.
